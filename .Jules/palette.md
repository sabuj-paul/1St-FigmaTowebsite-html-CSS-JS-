# Palette's Journal

## 2025-05-14 - Functional Anchors and Focus States
**Learning:** Landing page templates often leave CTA and sidebar links as placeholders (`#`), which breaks user expectations of navigation. Coupling functional anchors with `:focus-visible` styles and `aria-label` for icon-only buttons provides a significant accessibility boost with minimal code.
**Action:** Always verify that "Explore" or "Action" buttons in hero sections point to meaningful content anchors, and ensure mobile sidebars close automatically upon selection.
